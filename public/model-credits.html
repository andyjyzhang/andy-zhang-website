Built with a little help.

Physical part geometry is based on the LDraw.org Parts Library, with contributions by James Jessiman, Steve Bliss, Chris Dee, Nils Schmidt, Max Martin Richter, Orion Pobursky, Tore Eriksson, and other library authors.

Minifigures, vehicle parts, machinery dishes, furniture, lamps and other specialist molds are adapted from library part data. The rectangular brick, plate and tile catalog also uses official source IDs. Their surfaces are merged and quantized for browser rendering, while their proportions and molded details are retained.
